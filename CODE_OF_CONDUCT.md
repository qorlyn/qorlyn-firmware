
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Qorlyn is an open source project with a clear political mission: to make communication free, secure, anonymous, and accessible to those who need it.

Everyone who participates in this project—as a contributor, maintainer, or community member—commits to maintaining a respectful, professional, and safe environment for all, regardless of:

- Age
- Gender, gender identity or expression
- Sexual orientation
- Disability
- Ethnicity, nationality, origin
- Religion (or lack thereof)
- Level of technical experience
- Political views (as long as they are compatible with the project's mission)

We are not neutral. Qorlyn exists to protect people under surveillance, censorship, and war. But the fight for freedom does not justify toxic behavior within the community.

## Expected Behavior

- Respect. Treat others as you would like to be treated.
- Constructiveness. Criticisms must be technical, not personal.
- Patience. Contributors work in their free time. For free.
- Openness. Accept being wrong. Accept learning.
- Discretion. Do not ask for personal information without reason.
- Confidentiality. Do not share screenshots, logs, or other people's data.
- Consent. Do not tag people in public discussions without permission.
- Anonymity. Respect those who choose not to reveal their identity.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment of any kind (verbal, written, sexual).
- Discrimination based on age, gender, ethnicity, religion, disability.
- Threats or incitement to violence.
- Doxxing (revealing personal information of others without consent).
- Personal attacks (not technical, not constructive).
- Deliberate trolling.
- Spam or unsolicited self-promotion.
- Sexual content in any project context.
- Use of sexist, racist, homophobic, ableist, xenophobic language.
- Publishing unfixed vulnerabilities.
- Publishing private communications without permission.
- Impersonating other members or the project.

## Scope

This code applies to:

- All project spaces (GitHub repository, Discord, forum, email).
- Public or private events related to the project.
- Public interactions where you represent Qorlyn.
- Private communications related to the project.

It applies to everyone: maintainers, contributors, users, visitors.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to:

qorlyn@qorlyn.org

The report is confidential. It will be read only by the maintainers.

Include, if possible:
- What happened.
- When and where.
- Who was involved (if you wish).
- Any evidence (screenshots, logs).

You are not required to identify yourself. You may report anonymously. But an anonymous report is harder to verify.

## Consequences

Violations will be handled proportionally. Possible consequences, in increasing order:

1. Private warning. Explanation of what was wrong.
2. Public warning. If the behavior persists.
3. Temporary suspension. From 7 to 30 days, from all spaces.
4. Permanent ban. For serious or repeated violations.

Decisions are made by the maintainers, in good faith, after hearing all parties involved.

There is no appeal against a maintainer's decision. But you may request a review by writing to qorlyn@qorlyn.org.

## Enforcement Principles

- Proportionality. The consequence must be proportional to the harm.
- Context. Not all violations are equal.
- Good faith. We presume good faith unless proven otherwise.
- Privacy. Reports remain confidential.
- Transparency. When possible, we explain decisions.
- Consistency. The same rules for everyone.
